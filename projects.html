PROJECT CHECKLIST

Prepare before materials arrive

A little coordination can help reduce delays and avoid damaged materials. Use this general checklist with your project team:

Confirm quantities

Have your contractor estimate requirements from the approved plans and allow for the work sequence.

Plan delivery access

Check delivery timing, unloading space and safe access to the site before confirming an order.

Arrange dry storage

Prepare a clean, weather-protected space where cement can remain dry and be used in the order received.

Discuss your project requirements

Contact our team for product and supply enquiries. Structural specifications should always be confirmed by your project's qualified engineer.
